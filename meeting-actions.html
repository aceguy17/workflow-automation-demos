    What lands in your tools

    The problem

    Actions get agreed in meetings and then live in someone's notebook. Nobody is sure who owns what, follow-up emails go out days later, and the same items come up again next week.

    What the workflow does

    • Writes a short summary with decisions, risks and questions
    • Finds every action and who said they'd do it
    • Turns "by next Friday" into a real due date
    • Creates tasks and sends a follow-up to attendees

    In a real build

    • Trigger: Zoom, Teams or Google Meet transcript, Otter or Fireflies, or a notes doc
    • AI: Claude or OpenAI with a task schema and your team list
    • Tasks: Asana, Notion, Trello, ClickUp, Monday or Salesforce
    • Follow-up: Gmail or Outlook draft, plus a calendar invite for the next meeting

    Personal demo with fictional people and companies. The AI step is simulated in your browser with rule-based extraction so you can try your own notes. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
