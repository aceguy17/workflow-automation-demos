    What lands in your tools

    The problem

    Enquiries sit in an inbox for hours. Someone copies details into the CRM by hand, good leads wait as long as spam, and replies are slow and generic.

    What the workflow does

    • Pulls out name, company, contact, need, budget and timeline
    • Scores fit and urgency, filters out spam
    • Creates or updates the CRM record (no duplicates)
    • Alerts the right person and drafts a tailored reply

    In a real build

    • Trigger: website form, Gmail/Outlook, Facebook lead ads
    • AI: OpenAI or Claude API with structured output
    • CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or Zoho
    • Alerts: Slack, Teams or email. Replies stay drafts until a person approves them

    Personal demo with fictional people and companies. The AI steps are simulated in your browser with rule-based extraction so you can try it without an account. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. A client build connects to real systems and an LLM API.
