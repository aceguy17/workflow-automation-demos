    What lands in your tools

    The problem

    Every ticket lands in one queue. Someone reads each one to decide what it is and who should take it, urgent problems wait behind "how do I" questions, and agents write the same answers over and over.

    What the workflow does

    • Looks up the customer's plan and history
    • Works out the category, urgency and tone
    • Sets priority and SLA, routes to the right team
    • Drafts a reply from the matching help article and escalates outages

    In a real build

    • Trigger: Zendesk, Freshdesk, Help Scout, Gorgias, Salesforce Service Cloud or a shared inbox
    • AI: Claude or OpenAI with your categories and help articles
    • Lookup: CRM or billing system (HubSpot, Stripe, Shopify)
    • Escalation: Slack or Teams, with an on-call mention for P1

    Personal demo for a fictional software company ("Tallyhub") and fictional customers. The AI steps are simulated in your browser with rule-based classification. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
