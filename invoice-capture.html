    What lands in your tools

    The problem

    Someone opens every supplier email, downloads the PDF, types the figures into a spreadsheet or accounting system, and hopes they spot the duplicate or the wrong GST before it gets paid.

    What the workflow does

    • Reads the invoice attachment and extracts every field
    • Checks line totals, 10% GST and the ABN checksum
    • Blocks duplicates before they reach payment
    • Sends bills over your limit for approval and posts clean ones straight to the register

    In a real build

    • Trigger: Gmail or Outlook folder, or a shared drive
    • Reading: PDF text extraction, OCR for scans
    • AI: Claude or OpenAI with a fixed invoice schema
    • Output: Google Sheets, Excel, Xero or MYOB draft bills, plus a Slack or email exceptions report

    Personal demo with fictional suppliers and invoices. The AI step is simulated in your browser with rule-based extraction; the GST, total and ABN checks are real calculations. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
